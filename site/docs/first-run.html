MarDock has no icon of its own in the dock. It lives in the menu bar, and the welcome screen sets up the rest.

The welcome screen

You'll see it once. Each choice can be changed later in Settings.

ChoiceWhat it does
Bring over the apps from my current DockReplaces what's in MarDock with the apps in your macOS Dock. It starts ticked on a new install, and unticked if you've already arranged MarDock.
Hide the macOS Dock while MarDock is runningSaves your Dock's settings, then hides it. See macOS Dock.
Open MarDock at loginAdds MarDock to your login items.

Click Get Started to apply them. Closing the window counts as an answer too: nothing changes, and the screen doesn't come back.

Finding MarDock

MarDock's icon in the menu bar is the way in. From there you can:

  • Show or hide MarDock's dock
  • Hide or restore the macOS Dock
  • Turn Open at Login on or off
  • Open Settings, or check for updates
  • Quit

You can also right-click an empty part of the dock and choose MarDock Settings…. Opening MarDock again from Finder or Spotlight shows Settings too.

Finding a setting

Settings has a search field at the top of its sidebar. Press ⌘F and type what you're after, such as “login” or “hide”, and MarDock takes you to the setting and points it out.
