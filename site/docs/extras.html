Giving a permission

Turn an extra on and macOS asks once. After that it's a switch in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security. If a permission is missing, Settings says so next to the extra and takes you there.

Releases are signed ad-hoc for now, so macOS treats each new version as a new app. After an update you'll need to allow Accessibility and Screen Recording again.

What MarDock reads

Badges come from the macOS Dock, which keeps them even while it's hidden. Minimized windows are read by title only. Previews are small pictures of the app you're pointing at, shown and then dropped: nothing is kept or sent anywhere. More on privacy.
