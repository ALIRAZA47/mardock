All of this is in Settings ▸ Behaviour. Position and hiding are also in the dock's own menu.

Position

Put the dock along the left, bottom or right of the screen.

Display is either the main display, or Follow the pointer, which moves the dock to whichever display you push the pointer against.

Hiding

Automatically hideWhat happens
NeverThe dock stays in view.
AlwaysIt slides away, and comes back when you push the pointer against the screen edge.
When a window overlapsIt stays in view until the app you're using puts a window over it.

Reveal delay sets how long to wait at the edge, from 0 to 1.5 seconds. Show in full-screen spaces keeps the dock available in full-screen apps.

macOS doesn't let other apps reserve screen space the way its own Dock does, so maximised windows can extend under MarDock. When a window overlaps is made for that.

Contents

  • Show running apps that aren't kept in the dock
  • Show recently used apps, from 1 to 10 of them
  • Show the Trash

Clicking

Clicking the frontmost app hides it does what it says. With double-click shortcuts on, the app hides a moment after the click, once it's clear no second click is coming.
