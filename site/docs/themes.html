Change the theme in Settings ▸ Appearance, or right-click the dock and choose Theme. The preview at the top of Settings stays in view while you adjust things below it.

The seven themes

You can try all of them in the live demo.

Making your own

Choose New Theme in Settings ▸ Appearance. It starts from the theme you're using, and the editor changes the glass, tint, drifting colours, edge, neon glow, shadows and accent with a live preview. Nothing changes until you save.

Right-click one of your themes to edit, duplicate, export or delete it. Themes are small files you can share: use Export… to save one, and Import… to add one someone sent you.

Icons

SettingRange
Size32 to 112 pt
Magnification amount1.1× to 2.6×
Magnification reach1.5 to 6, how far along the dock it spreads

If the dock wouldn't fit on the screen, MarDock shrinks the icons to fit.

Details

  • Running indicators: glow, dots, lines or none.
  • Accent colour: the theme's, the system's, or one of your choosing.
  • App names on hover
  • Reflections, for a dock along the bottom. Mirror always has them.
  • Dim icons of hidden apps

Motion

  • Bounce when opening apps
  • Ripple when clicking
  • Animation speed, from 0.5× to 2×

With Reduce Motion turned on in macOS's Accessibility settings, MarDock skips bounces and particle effects automatically. It also respects Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast.
