If you've used the macOS Dock, most of this will feel familiar. These are the parts worth knowing.

Clicking

Do thisTo get this
Click an appOpens it, or brings it forward
Double-click an appIts shortcuts
⌘-click an appShows it in Finder
⌥-click an appHides it, or shows it again
Right-click an appIts menu
Right-click the dockThemes, position, hiding, magnification, profiles and adding items

You can also have a click on the frontmost app hide it: turn on Clicking the frontmost app hides it in Settings ▸ Behaviour.

Dragging

  • Add an app: drag it in from Finder.
  • Reorder: drag a tile along the dock.
  • Remove: drag a tile off the dock. It leaves in a puff of smoke.
  • Open a document in an app: drop it on that app's tile.

An app's menu

Right-click an app for Shortcuts, Show or Hide, Show in Finder, Keep in Dock or Remove from Dock, and Change Icon. Running apps also have Quit, which turns into Force Quit while you hold ⌥.

Folders

Keep a folder in the dock and its tile shows the newest files. Click it to fan them out, or see them in a grid if there are many. Click a file to open it, and click anywhere else to close the stack.

Right-click a folder to display it as a stack or a plain folder, and to sort by date added, date modified or name. Add one with Add Folder… in the dock's menu.

The Share tile

Drop files on the Share tile to send them with AirDrop, Mail or Messages. Add it with Add Share Tile in the dock's menu.

The Trash

Click the Trash to open it. Right-click it for Empty Trash…, which asks the same question Finder does. Hold ⌥ to skip the question. MarDock asks Finder to do the emptying, as the macOS Dock does, so macOS asks once for permission to control Finder.

Recent apps

Apps you've used lately appear in their own section, so a running or recent app is easy to find even if you don't keep it in the dock. Right-click one to keep it, or to remove it from the list. Change how many appear in Settings ▸ Behaviour.
