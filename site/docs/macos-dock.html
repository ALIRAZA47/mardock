MarDock can sit alongside the macOS Dock or replace it. Replacing it is one switch, at the top of Settings ▸ General.

Replace the macOS Dock

Turn on Replace the macOS Dock, and MarDock saves your Dock's settings and hides it. macOS has no switch for this, so MarDock uses four of the Dock's own settings together:

Dock settingWhy
autohide onKeeps the Dock off the screen.
autohide-delay 1000 secondsStops it coming back when the pointer rests at the edge.
no-bouncing onOtherwise an app asking for attention brings the Dock up to bounce its icon.
allow-display-switching offOtherwise it moves to whichever display the pointer pushes down against.

The macOS Dock restarts once to pick these up, and MarDock keeps them in place from then on, including after a restart.

While the Dock is hidden, apps can't bounce for attention anywhere: macOS doesn't tell other apps about those requests, so MarDock can't show them. Notification badges still work, as an extra.

Getting it back

Turn the switch off, or choose Restore the macOS Dock in the menu bar menu. Quitting MarDock restores it too, unless you turn off Restore the macOS Dock when MarDock quits. Restoring puts back exactly what was there before.

If MarDock ever can't, say after a crash, reopen it or run:

defaults delete com.apple.dock autohide-delay
defaults delete com.apple.dock no-bouncing
defaults delete com.apple.dock allow-display-switching
defaults write com.apple.dock autohide -bool false
killall Dock

Tune the macOS Dock

Settings ▸ macOS Dock also changes the Dock's own settings, whether you've hidden it or not:

  • Minimize effect: genie, scale or suck
  • Minimize windows into their app's icon
  • Show suggested and recent apps
  • Animate opening apps
  • Show indicators for open apps
  • Make icons of hidden apps translucent
  • Scroll up on an icon to show its windows
  • Auto-hide delay and animation speed

Applying restarts the macOS Dock, which takes a moment. Reset to macOS Defaults removes every change, except that a hidden Dock stays hidden.

Minimized windows

Minimized windows still go to the hidden macOS Dock. Either turn on Minimized windows in the dock in Extras, or turn on Minimize windows into their app's icon here and click the app to bring them back.
