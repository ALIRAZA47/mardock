Arrange the dock directly by dragging, or use Settings ▸ Apps for everything at once.

What's in the dock

Finder always comes first. After that, your items go in the order you give them. In Settings, drag rows to reorder and use the minus button to remove one.

ItemWhat it is
AppAdd with Add Apps…, or drag one in from Finder.
SpacerAn empty gap between apps.
DividerA line, with an optional name that shows when you point at it.
Share tileSends files you drop on it to AirDrop, Mail or Messages.
FolderShows its newest files when you click it.

To start from what you already have, use Import from macOS Dock. It replaces the apps in the current profile.

Custom icons

Drop an image on an app's icon in Settings ▸ Apps, or click the icon to choose one. On the dock, right-click the app and choose Change Icon…. Use Original Icon puts it back.

Profiles

A profile is a set of apps and items you can switch between, such as Work and Personal. Switch from the dock's menu or from Settings ▸ Apps. A new profile starts as a copy of the current dock.

A Focus can switch profiles for you. See Shortcuts, Siri and Focus.
