A dock that moves like it's made of something real.

MarDock replaces the macOS Dock with physically based magnification, seven themes and shortcuts on every app. It runs sandboxed, with no network access.

The real app, recorded on every build

Whatever is under your pointer stays under it.

Most docks scale icons and re-centre the row, so the icon you're aiming at slides away. MarDock warps the dock's axis around the pointer instead.

Magnification, bounces, presses and auto-hide are all real springs, solved in closed form, so they stay smooth at any refresh rate, 120 Hz included.

Themes

Seven looks, or make your own.

The theme editor changes the glass, tint, drifting colours, edge, neon glow, shadows and accent with a live preview. Themes are small files you can share.

Shortcuts

Double-click any app for its shortcuts.

A small panel opens above the tile with the app's own New and Open commands and their keyboard shortcuts, the websites, files and Shortcuts you added for it, and Hide, Quit and the rest.

Prefer the keyboard? ⌃⌥1 to ⌃⌥9 open the first nine apps in the dock.

macOS Dock

One switch replaces the macOS Dock.

It stays hidden when an app bounces for attention and when your pointer crosses displays. Your original Dock settings are saved first and put back exactly when you turn it off or quit.

Search finds any setting, and MarDock opens at login.

Privacy & Security

A dock doesn't need your data, so MarDock can't reach it.

App Sandbox
On. It can't read your files or other apps' data.
Network access
None. macOS blocks every connection from the dock.
Permissions
None by default. Extras ask only if you turn them on.
Hardened Runtime
On, with no exceptions, on every executable.
Third-party code
None. Pure Swift, AppKit and Core Animation.
Tracking
None. No analytics, crash reporting or accounts.
